## 2. The key result: survivorship explains the momentum edge

A4 (60% A1b momentum + 40% A5 ETF rotation) passed every §3.2 bar on today's constituents: CAGR
26.2%, Sharpe 1.49, maxDD 20.6%. Frozen and re-run on the TRUE as-of index membership (a stock
tradable only while it was a member; book and benchmark both on that universe):

| | A4 as-of | Passive benchmark as-of (equal-weight members + 60/40 NIFTYBEES/GOLDBEES) |
|---|---|---|
| Net CAGR | 20.4% | 17.9% |
| Net Sharpe | 1.22 | **1.33** |
| Max drawdown | 19.6% | 27.1% |
| Worst month | -10.1% | |

Once today's winners are no longer handed to it, momentum stock selection adds return but no
risk-adjusted value over holding the same universe passively. The return that remains comes from
the market itself, the mid-cap tilt of equal weighting, and gold. Its one real contribution is
drawdown control. A4 is parked by the rule fixed before the stress ran.

## 3. What that implies

1. The dependable return source in 2017-2024 was **exposure** (Indian equity, mid-caps, gold),
   not selection or timing. Exposure can be bought with index ETFs at a fraction of the cost and
   turnover, and ETFs are survivorship-free by construction.
2. Timing rules (trend filters, rotation) earn their keep through **drawdown**, not return: A5 had a
   13.7% max drawdown against 35.5% for 60/40, at a similar CAGR.
3. The 18% CAGR bar in §3.2 is above what passive exposure delivered on honest data (about 18%
   equal-weight, 11-14% for investable ETF mixes). A strategy that clears it has to add skill on
   top of exposure, and nothing has, after about 100 trials across three tracks at this capital.

## 4. Options for the operator (a goal decision, not a statistical one)

  gold, cash) with a trend filter for drawdown control, held to a **core** bar: net CAGR above
  60/40 buy-and-hold, lower maximum drawdown, and the §3.2 shape bars (months, worst month, §3.4).
  Expected long-run return is about 11-15% a year, and it is honest about that. Growth to the

Either way the §2 rules, the costs and the vault stay as they are.
