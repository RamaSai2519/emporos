Reading it:
 * The structure is a small credit against a much larger capped loss: the average credit is Rs 426 (k=1.0) or Rs 195 (k=1.5) on a wing that can lose about Rs 3,950-4,350. The 2x-credit stop and the 50% target make the average loss about 5x the average win, so the strategy needs about an 83% win rate before any cost. It has 82%: the volatility risk premium in this structure is about zero even with free fills (gross Rs 111 per spread against Rs 109 of charges; filter off is negative before charges: -13).
 * Fees: Rs 109 per spread (four leg-orders at Rs 20 brokerage plus statutory charges) is 26% of the k=1.0 credit and 56% of the k=1.5 credit.
 * Slippage: the benchmark's one tick plus 0.5% of the premium takes about Rs 79 per spread (gross 111 -> 32), 18% of the credit; the adverse scenario takes about Rs 164 more, and with 1.5x charges every arm is deeply negative. The result is therefore decided by an ASSUMPTION (no bid-ask history exists; D7 would measure it), but even at zero slippage no arm clears the bars: net CAGR would still be about zero.
 * The stop: 17 of 93 spreads (k=1.0 filter on) hit the 2x stop and lost Rs -19,657 in total; 73 profit targets earned Rs +12,982; 3 time exits lost Rs -473.
 * The regime filter reduced trips (200 -> 93) and the loss (Rs -18,331 -> -7,149 for k=1.0), but it removed exposure, it did not create an edge.
